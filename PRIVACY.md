# Logic Analyze privacy policy

Logic Analyze does not collect, store on any server, transmit or sell personal data. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no network service. Captures, settings and files stay on your Mac unless you choose to share them.

The optional MCP feature (off by default) lets AI assistants that you run on the same Mac ask Logic Analyze to capture, measure and decode. Logic Analyze sends the results only to that local assistant. What the assistant does with them, including sending them to its own service, is governed by that assistant's terms and privacy policy.
