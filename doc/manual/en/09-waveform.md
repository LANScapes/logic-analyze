# Examine the waveform

After a capture, the waveform area shows the data of each channel as a waveform.

## Move the waveform to the left or to the right

Use one of these methods:

- **Drag.** Put the pointer on the waveform. Hold the left mouse button and move the mouse to the left or to the right.
- **Fast drag.** Drag the waveform fast and release the mouse button. The waveform continues to move. Then it moves more slowly and stops. To turn this function on or off, use **Waveform Scroll with Mouse Drag** in the display options. Refer to [Install and start the app](02-install.md).
- **Scroll bar.** Drag the scroll bar at the bottom of the window.
- **Keyboard.** Press `Page Up` or `Page Down`. The waveform moves by one window width.

## Zoom in and zoom out

Use one of these methods:

- **Mouse wheel.** Put the pointer on the waveform and turn the mouse wheel. The pointer stays at the middle of the zoom.
- **Area zoom.** Hold the right mouse button and drag across the waveform. Release the button. The app zooms in on the area that you selected.
- **Full view.** Double-click the waveform with the right mouse button. The app shows all the data. Do the double-click with the right mouse button again to go back to the previous zoom.
- **Keyboard.** Press `←` to zoom in. Press `→` to zoom out.

## Find a pattern

The search finds a pattern of levels and edges on the channels.

1. Click **Search** on the toolbar or press `R`. The search bar opens at the bottom of the window.
2. Click the search field. The **Search Options** window opens.
3. For each channel, type one of the characters `X`, `0`, `1`, `R`, `F` or `C`. [Triggers](07-trigger.md) gives the function of each character.
4. Click **OK**.
5. Click the arrow buttons in the search bar to go to the previous result or to the next result.

## Change a channel

Each channel has a label on the left of the waveform area. The label shows the color, the name and the number of the channel.

![The channel label: color, name and number](../figures/channel-label.png)

### Change the color

1. Click the color area on the channel label.
2. Select a color.
3. Click **OK**.

### Change the name

1. Click the name on the channel label.
2. Type a new name.
3. Press `Return`.

### Change the sequence of the channels

When you put the pointer on a channel label, the pointer shows an arrow. Use one of these methods to move the channel:

- Hold the left mouse button on the label and drag the channel up or down. Release the button at the new position.
- Click the label to select the channel. Move the mouse. The channel moves with the mouse. Click again to release the channel at the new position.
- Hold `Command` and click more than one label. Move the mouse. The selected channels move with the mouse. Click again to release them.
